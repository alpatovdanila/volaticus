# DEATHTERRA — Design Notes

Running notes on the *intent* behind mechanics — the "why" and how something is meant to
feel or be used, not the "how it's built" (that lives in [GAME.md](GAME.md) and
[ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md)).

## Ultimates

### ZOMBIE EATER

An ultimate that lasts until it kills 10 zombies. When used correctly, it's overpowered
because it can endlessly kill other enemies, but it's a shotgun, so it's difficult to miss
zombies that roam in hordes and are always close to you, so to use it, you should always be
closer to the more dangerous enemy and farther away from the zombies.

If skillfully applied, it could be an IMBALANCE weapon.

- **Shotgun:** 12 pellets, relatively wide spread, fire rate 50% slower than the main gun.
- **Counts zombies exclusively:** any other enemy can be shot without increasing the
  counter — that's the point. Only zombie kills spend the ultimate.
- **Skill expression:** position so the dangerous target sits in the cone and the zombie
  horde does not, and the ult keeps paying out.

## Enemies

### Zombies

Zombies' purpose is to be not very dangerous but annoying, getting in the way of dealing
with more dangerous enemies.

## Movement & tactics

The movement is the skill ceiling, but that doesn't necessarily mean we should go the
archero way. We need to make movement matter in a more tactical way — choosing cover or
elevation. There should be moves that give a real tactical advantage: for example, move
behind some destructible cover to buy yourself time against a big horde, or — or what?

Need to think about more ways to make movement more intentional. The player COULD choose to
move not only reactionarily (bullet-hell dodging), but also more tactically.

**WHAT INSTRUMENTS SHOULD WE PROVIDE?** (open question)

- Cover — line-of-sight breaks (interior walls exist now; bolts and target-selection respect
  them). Destructible cover that buys time is a candidate.
- Elevation — high ground for range/visibility advantage.
